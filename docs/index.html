Texas Grid Load Forecast

Day-ahead hourly electricity demand for three ERCOT weather zones, forecast from load history, weather and the calendar, plus an estimate of how much demand Hurricane Harvey removed once the cooler weather is accounted for. All forecasts shown are for 2017, which no model saw in training.

Grid strain by zone and day

Forecast daily peak as a share of each zone's 99th-percentile hourly load in the training years. Darker means closer to, or beyond, historical extremes.

Forecast error by model

Mean absolute percentage error on the 2017 test period, all zones. Lower is better.

How load responds to temperature

Average load at each temperature, as a share of the zone's mean load, training years.

Hurricane Harvey: change in Coast zone daily load

Percent change against expected load, by day. The raw line ignores weather; the adjusted line removes what the cool, wet weather alone explains.

Load: ERCOT hourly native load by weather zone. Weather: hourly city observations (OpenWeatherMap feed). Forecasts use observed weather for the target day in place of a weather forecast, so errors in live use would be larger. Method and limits: see the repository README.
